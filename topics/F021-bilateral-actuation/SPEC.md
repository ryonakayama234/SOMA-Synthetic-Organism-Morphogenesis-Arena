# F021 — Bilateral Actuation / DESIGN ONLY

[Research question #72](../../issues/72) · [PLAN](../../experiments/F021/PLAN.md) · [UNEXECUTED findings](../../findings/F021-validation.md).

## Unknown
Previous F018–F020 force only A; B can be carried, claim energy, and pay link fees, but not use its battery to generate motion. What changes in dynamics, own resource ownership and per-cell net energy when **both physically identical cells can drive from their own batteries**? Can linking help, hinder, or be neutral without programming a connection reward?

## Hypotheses and falsifiers
H21a (mechanics): linked 1D ideal bodies obey (mA+mB)a = FA_applied + FB_applied + any specified external terms; unlinked cells accelerate independently. For mA=mB=1kg, FA=+2N, FB=0/+2/−2N, predicted linked a=+1/+2/0 m/s², respectively. Internal ideal constraint impulses cancel in the whole-system momentum balance. Wrong signs, double-counted reaction, or free internal thrust refute the technical implementation.

H21b (ecology): two independently owned motor contributions and batteries *can* change path-first patch claimant / motor acceptance / each cell's net balance under fixed pre-observation trajectory-blind field rules. **There is no predicted positive frequency or bilateral benefit.** No change, negative benefit, and asymmetric effects are valid results.

H21c (confound controls): matched investigator-fixed linked/unlinked worlds; A only, B only, both codirectional, both opposing, neither; mirror signs; high/zero/low own batteries; no patches; no harvest; motor refusal; reversed identities. A/B energy is not transferable except through separately admitted, explicitly audited mechanics. Outside option must include idle and actually feasible own actions. Do not infer voluntary attachment or cooperative policy from an offline counterfactual.

H21d (evidence): archived stepwise forces, positional states, ledger events, patch ownership and claims can be verified by a **separately written saved-data oracle**, including 3.11/3.12, negative adversarial rehashing and byte-identical two-root rerun.

## Model boundaries
New versioned F021 orchestration ONLY. Keep existing F001–F020 v1 implementations, schemas, frozen worlds and outcomes immutable. 1D, ideal connection, action commands supplied by investigator, externally effective motor forces, no actual ground reaction or realistic holding current, no dynamic approach/docking, learning, self-assembly, tool acquisition, 2D/3D or evolution. No direct reward for linking. True patch positions or counterfactual trajectories NEVER enter cell action inputs.

## Pre-freeze critical blocker: simultaneous motor-work admission
With a linked pair, each motor's positive F005 work depends on the **resultant** motion and therefore on the OTHER motor being accepted. Independent sequential checks would introduce arbitrary A-first/B-first privilege; naive simultaneous acceptance may create circular eligibility. **Do not implement or freeze the primary F021 experiment until a deterministic, symmetry-auditable simultaneous admission rule is derived and demonstrated independently**, including tie / zero displacement / opposing motors / insufficient batteries / time discretization cases. This is a design uncertainty, not an accepted API. Prefer a physically interpretable rule and report any necessary idealizations explicitly. A pre-observation feasibility calculation is not a stochastic SOMA result.
