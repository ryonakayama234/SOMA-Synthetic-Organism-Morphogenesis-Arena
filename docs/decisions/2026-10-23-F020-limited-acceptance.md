## Independent critical reanalysis of frozen primary saved worlds

All counts below are **paired contrasts on identical exogenous fields**; 32 SHA-derived environmental seeds recur over signs, fee, radii, densities and batteries. These 3,072 contrasts are highly correlated, never independent population trials.

| Each cell's starting balance | YY: changed movement /512 | NN: changed movement /512 | YY / NN: claimant-map changed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 75,000 µJ | 33 | 0 | 2 / 0 |
| 250,000 µJ | 82 | 206 | 6 / 56 |
| 4,000,000 µJ | 0 | 0 | 0 / 0 |
| **Total** | **115 /1536** | **206 /1536** | **8 /56** |

- **321/3,072 (10.45%)** pairs have positive extra displacement in the imposed A motor direction; median directional displacement among the 321 is **0.1875m**, range **0.0625–0.75m**. 27/32 original resource seed blocks contain at least one changed comparison, five contain none. No frequentist population inference from 3,072 pseudoreplicates is supported.
- **64/3,072 (2.08%)** pairs also change later resource access, all within the five-patch factor group (zero in two-patch group); compared directly at same topology they yield **74 additional A patch claims of 1,000,000µJ each, no lost A claims in this fixed sample, and zero B claim differences**. Other fields/time horizons need not behave this way.
- **Post-observation energy-cost counterargument:** Among the 321 trajectory-changed matched pairs, **263** have *lower A final net µJ* under feedback, **36** equal, **22** higher. B net differences are zero for **all 3,072** feedback-vs-deferred comparisons, because no B motor and its resource-claim map did not change. Therefore **motion access ≠ increased remaining energy**, let alone both cells' joint payoff. Extra work can use more battery than newly gained resources. No bilateral-positive additional-net comparison exists for this counterfactual endpoint.
- **Exploratory fee stratification:** 75k YY + no fee yields 33/256 changed, but 75k YY + 25k upfront per-cell fee yields 0/256: A can afford 62.5k first linked work with 75k, but cannot from 50k. For 250k YY the changed comparison counts are 49/256 at fee0 and 33/256 at fee25k. NN's fee is never charged, hence duplicated identical NN contrasts between fee factor levels.
- **Exploratory geometry:** 2 patches produced **128/1536** motor-sensitive pairs but **0** changed claimant maps; 5 patches produced **193/1536** motor-sensitive pairs and all **64** claimant-map changes. This indicates constrained 1s horizon/geometry sensitivity, not a universal threshold.
- **Direction:** ±2N signs were both present and not equally sensitive (sign−: 210/1536; sign+: 111/1536); random patches are on a finite fixed grid and the joint initial positions are asymmetric, so never describe source data as rotational/spatially isotropic.

### Real archived scenario example (not a selected primary statistic)

Primary scene `s01-n5-r060-sign-1-bal0250000-fee00000`, **unlinked NN, original seed 1**. Patch P1 at x≈−0.175m with radius 0.06m is first touched by A at step1. Both mode forks agree through step1 (A x = −0.125m, pays 125,000µJ). On feedback branch A now has 1.125J and is authorized to drive at steps 2 and 3, reaching xA(k4)=**−1.125m**, and it also first claims patch P4 near −0.95m at step4. On deferred-credit branch A retains **0.125J spendable** during the episode, motors k2..4 are rejected and inertia yields xA(k4)=**−0.5m**, with P4 unclaimed. Resource claim time and final position were independently recomputed by Wolfram using exact rational motion/first-entry, not by trusting the plotted producer trajectory. This example exhibits the loop directly but is selected *after* viewing data and is NOT an independent confirmation.

The 75k NN zero is a **structural boundary**: starting 75k cannot pay the unlinked A first-step 125k cost; stationary paths cannot reach non-initial patches. The 4M no-effect group is another **expected null**: NN consumes 2J and YY 1J for uninterrupted four steps, below the battery ceiling. Thus a substantial portion of zeros was designed as falsifying controls; it should not be interpreted as a failed ecological discovery.

## Limitations, contrary interpretations, and use boundaries

- F007 prescribes an effective **external** A-only motor force in ideal 1D. The linked B is pulled passively and has no independent controller, energy transfer, mutual choice or active joint action.
- YY/NN are researcher-assigned fixed consent outcomes, not learned joining decisions. The generator's patch lattice and finite 1s horizon are model choices and do not represent arbitrary natural ecology.
- The 321 motor-sensitive contrasts should be treated as **within-model causal counterfactuals**, not independently randomized organisms or survival measures. The 64 patch differences are downstream, but extra future motor work often costs more than it harvests.
- This internal AI review plus source-independent saved-data verifier is NOT independent human peer review. Shared incorrect assumptions can survive the computational checks; software acceptance does not imply external validity.
