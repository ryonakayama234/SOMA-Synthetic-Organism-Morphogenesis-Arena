# Research decision 2026-10-23 — choose F020 metabolic feedback

**Repo audit baseline:** `main` SHA `7de755c79fed3b806417da5c15f0115ea7ac04c8`, all 19 post-main F001–F019 workflows succeeded. F019 #64 / #65 / #66 merged. ## Compared candidates (research-value × dependency × falsifiability, qualitative)
1. **F020 energy-feedback loop — CHOSEN:** F019 harvest has no motor consequences, so closing energy→motion→future encounter is the smallest causal bridge toward viability studies. Reuses F002/F005/F007/F018/F019, while adding one new capability.
2. **Symmetric independent A/B actuation — later:** scientifically important to remove A-only asymmetry and test cooperative movement, but changes mechanical and energy semantics. Analyze after the closed-loop ledger is validated.
3. **Mid-run join/detach/rejoin — later:** tests time-varying body boundary directly, but premature without genuine two-sided action, physical docking conditions and a causal survival opportunity.
4. **Delayed reciprocity / repeated cooperation — later:** currently confounded by fixed motor ownership and finite one-shot horizon. Must never silently implement externally omniscient policies.
5. **V001 viewer — parallel tooling:** improves researcher observability and can consume saved F020 evidence once schema is stable; separate non-scientific PRs #25/#26 already open, do not conflate with experimental adoption.

## Strategic scientific reasoning
F019 demonstrates morphology-dependent ownership but **not** positive bilateral joining incentives: 580/1792 claimant changes, 0/512 bilateral-positive in the frozen samples. Its fixed rich battery means harvested resource never changes future actions. A survival ecology requires a non-vacuous closed causal circuit before learning/free choice of morphology is interpretable. F020 tests only **the physical accounting and causal feedback**; it does NOT assert spontaneous joining or cellular metabolism.

**Wolfram exact fraction sanity check:** in F007's 1D equal-mass model, for u=2N and dt=1/4s the first-step work is 1/16J linked and 1/8J solo, so 75,000µJ lies between gates; no field-specific result used in this choice.
