# 2026-10-25 — Select the first 2D Encounter Lab

**Decision:** Following F021.v1 limited adoption, select F022 as a narrowly scoped 2D passive contact/encounter instrument, not an endogenous assembly experiment. Owner delegated GitHub issue and research planning; no pre-observation freeze and no experiment/adoption at this stage. Parent [#51](https://github.com/ryonakayama234/SOMA-Synthetic-Organism-Morphogenesis-Arena/issues/51), selected [#77](https://github.com/ryonakayama234/SOMA-Synthetic-Organism-Morphogenesis-Arena/issues/77).

## Why this gap
Existing F021 permits mathematically assigned initial YY/NN 1D link configurations, independently owned ideal external-effective motors, finite patch claims, per-cell payoff contrasts. It cannot observe a freely moving cell meeting another one; 1D can falsely simplify grazing/near-miss geometry. Before asking whether individuals choose to join, the simulator needs credible geometry that makes an encounter possible **without prescribing a bond**. F021's 0 bilateral positive vs idle-inclusive outside is a warning against rewarding attachment by fiat; it is not a proof that cooperation cannot arise.

## Candidate comparison (project value / core fit / confounds / effort)
1. **F022 2D contact world — chosen**: establishes the missing encounter mechanism; analytically testable without adding motives; medium engineering effort, high future reusability. Does not yet show emergence.
2. 1D local join/leave policy — deferred: useful but forces an artificial opportunity to encounter, entangles policy and fixed topology.
3. Finish V001 viewer — valuable parallel maintenance candidate: already implemented in Draft PRs #25–26, unmerged because browser usability, independent review and formal admission are outstanding. Do not duplicate its 1D F007 replay as F022 code.
4. Grounded force/electric actuator model — important later: bigger physical commitments, should specify only after baseline 2D contact dynamics is trustworthy.
5. 20-cell learned/novelty-driven colony — long-term ambition: too many new assumptions, obscures causal effect of contact and individual outcomes.

## Decision reversal conditions
If the 2D contact kernel cannot pass its conservation/continuity audit without uncontrolled numerical artifacts, stop and revisit collision modelling rather than add control, learning or a graphical veneer. If a novel local joining model becomes interpretable without new spatial physics, document the evidence and reconsider ranking at the next research selection, preserving this decision history.

## The architectural implication
Keep world-truth physics, local-observation boundary, archival/oracle, and researcher viewer separate. Prefer source-controlled versioned data and searchable diagrams/findings. One scientific ability per Issue; no retroactive change to F021 or V001 proofs, no automatic main merge. Success is **a credible experimental world**, not desired-looking organism behavior.
