# Future research note — 一時的な身体構成で資源・機能へのアクセスを変える

**Idea capture only, not an F019 requirement, frozen hypothesis, action API, scientific result, or commitment to a particular implementation.** This note preserves search terms and distinctions for future AI investigators. [Research charter](../PROJECT.md) / [F019 design](../../topics/F019-resource-ecology/SPEC.md) / [Long-horizon body emergence Issue #51](https://github.com/ryonakayama234/SOMA-Synthetic-Organism-Morphogenesis-Arena/issues/51).

## Core intuition (candidate, not conclusion)

A modular organism might address resource scarcity in two physically different ways: **move/transport the resource to the needy cell** (internal transfer, metabolism, pipes) OR **change the connection topology so that the needy cell itself reaches and claims resource**. These may coexist; neither should be declared universally natural or efficient. Their comparison depends on transport losses, travel distance, motor work, latch/unlatch costs, mechanical constraints, energy scarcity and latency.

Connectivity can change **who gets a finite patch** even with identical resource placement and external drive. This access/ownership effect does not automatically imply bilateral consent, shared identity or cooperation. It is the appropriately narrow subject of F019; the ideas below go beyond F019.

## Future candidate questions — separate issues, not one bundled feature

1. **Time-varying body topology / 可変結合・分離・再結合:** With independent cell actuation, local sensors, actual contact and nonzero switching costs, can attachment edges emerge and disappear based on individual survival opportunities? A conceptual graph is `G(t) = (V_cells ∪ V_objects, E(t))`; it is NOT an implemented physics engine or proposed F019 schema. Require truly observable local inputs, energy/latency constraints and unlinked controls.
2. **Long-horizon repeated cooperation / 互恵・時間的協調:** Could an individually costly short-term join be worthwhile through future role reversal? Distinguish actual learned policy with observed history from an investigator's omniscient discounted-payoff example; uncertainty, exploitation/free-riding and irreversible loss need controls.
3. **Transfer versus relocation / 代謝輸送と身体再配置:** Compare cell-to-cell energy/resource transfer against rearranging the receiving cell to the patch, and against doing neither. No engineered hard-coded benefit for a chosen route, and no predetermined feeder/receiver role.
4. **Temporary environmental objects / 道具の暫定取り込み:** A passive found object might alter reach, leverage, load bearing or transport when connected, then be detached. Test physical affordances and costs; an object is not an automatically beneficial 'tool' just because a graph edge was created. Separate physical object incorporation from communication, organismality and symbolic tool-use claims.
5. **Individuality / 個体境界:** A temporally recurring coalition is not necessarily one organism. Independently operationalize functional persistence, resource accounting, self-maintenance, autonomy/boundary, exchange and joint versus individual viability, without presupposing a 'brain', 'hand', 'donor', 'receiver' or fixed organ.

## Required future evidence boundaries

- Researcher-level world truth, oracle counterfactuals and full maps must not leak to cell controllers.
- Track per-cell energy and action costs separately from aggregate gains; allow profitable nonjoining, unilateral loss and failures as legitimate outcomes.
- Compare matched exogenous environments and ablate connectivity, actuation, resource transfer and object availability independently.
- Do not claim evolution absent heredity, variation and selection, or organismality absent specific operational evidence.
- Preserve one hypothesis per Issue, freeze experimental plan before results, and synchronize architecture, data contracts and append-only findings with any later implementation.

## Not in F019

F019 remains **fixed initial 1D connect-or-not, A-only effective motor, first-entry finite resource depletion, paired counterfactual accounting**. It contains NO mid-run detachment/rejoin, independent B motor, learned consent, internal metabolic transfer, external-object attachment, 2D geometry or organism-level identity. Future AI should *not* retrofit these features into F019 merely because this note is linked.
