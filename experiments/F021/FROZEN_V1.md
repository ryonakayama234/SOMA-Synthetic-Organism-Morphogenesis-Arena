# F021 frozen preregistration v1 (2026-10-24, before first F021 primary world)

**Status: frozen parameters and theoretical contract only.** F021 source and empirical outcomes are not yet created. Supersedes the earlier candidate-only parts of SPEC/PLAN prospectively, without rewriting historical notes. Parent: Issue #72; design PR #73.

## Scientific question and boundaries

Does giving **both independent cells independently charged ideal external-effective motors** change attachment-dependent A/B resource ownership and private net energy relative to one-sided F020 control? Only investigator-assigned initial YY (attached) versus NN (unattached) in fixed 1D. No learned policy, voluntary assembly, real physical ground or electrical holding cost. Outcomes may be positive, negative, zero or arbitration-dependent; none is treated as a reward target.

## Simulator convention, frozen before evidence

- 2 cells, mass 1 kg each; initial position A=0m, B=0.1m, velocity both 0; YY link length 0.1m, NN disconnected and without collision. dt=0.25s, 4 steps, no drag. Semi-implicit Euler positions and velocities from F001/F007; applied motor forces are external effective forces, source of reaction not modeled.
- Two commands independently owned: A=+2N or -2N; B in {0, A, -A}. The command sign is fixed over the four steps. Each cell's positive motor work is the **F005** time integral of max(0, force * linearly interpolated velocity), with cost ceil(1e6*work) µJ; no integer µJ overspend, no cross-cell energy transfer. A motor is not eligible at zero balance even if nominal work is zero.
- On YY, A and B velocity updates use total applied forces and total mass, while opposite link constraint forces sum to zero. NN computes independent motion. A rejected motor contributes zero force; inertia continues.
- At each step BEFORE motion enumerate applied-command subsets of active nonzero commands (A+B, A-only, B-only, none). For each subset, predict *joint* resulting step with the corresponding applied forces, recompute both work charges, and declare feasible only if each member has balance > 0 and charge <= its *own* balance. Choose feasible subset of maximum cardinality **if unique**. If multiple tied maximum-cardinality feasible subsets, select the empty subset, log `ambiguous_maximal`, and separately preserve the alternative feasible subsets for off-world sensitivity analysis. No silent A/B priority; this conservative fail-closed *arbitration assumption* is not natural agency. The tie selection is an investigative model limitation, NOT evidence that both cells prefer idleness. Off-world frozen sensitivity probes explicitly force A-only and B-only in the exact 80,000µJ calibration, and report tie counts in the primary set. Changing the rule later requires f021.v2 and a new preregistration.
- At end of step k: deduct each accepted motor's own work via F002 ledger; determine first entry of each finite patch (F019 first_entry) from actual start/end position on each cell path; depletion is per branch and first winner takes all. Tie at equal entry fraction resolved by (fraction, patch_id, cell_id), A before B, as documented F019; award the winning cell its own resource, available to that cell's motor at k+1, never at the same step. Join fee deducted from each cell at step 0 for YY only. No idle/holding energy in this ideal model.
- No divergence in data/physics APIs of F001–F020; implement `f021.v1` separately. Experimental truth/paired counterfactual result is never supplied to a cell policy. Here actions are externally prescribed, not learned. Every invalid, NaN, over-budget or malformed input must fail closed.

## Frozen primary grid

- Reuse trajectory-blind F019 field_for(seed, count, radius): seed=0..31 (32 **environment blocks**, not independent observation count), patch count {2,5}, radius {.02,.06}m, same 1,000,000µJ per patch, original 0.025m SHA256 spatial lattice. No outcome-based filtering or hand placement.
- A sign {-1,+1} times 2N; B relative force {-A,0,+A}; arm {YY,NN}; **both cells initially** {80,000, 250,000, 4,000,000}µJ; per-cell join fee {0,25,000}µJ (charged only YY).
- Primary grid: 32 × 2 × 3 × 2 × 3 × 2 × 2 × 2 = **4,608 archived worlds**; same fields are counterfactually reused, so the 4,608 are NOT 4,608 independent biological replicates. The arm / B-force / fee scenarios are paired within each seed/field and budget.
- F020 comparison cases A-only, YY/NN, limited energy are controls. No synthetic favorable patches in primary grid.
- **Calibration only (excluded from ecological sample)**: linked (+2,-2), (+2,0), (+2,+2) at high budget versus exact acceleration and work; zero and high budget; mirror labels/sign; no-resource; zero-command; budget=80,000µJ on each with A=B=+2N: both simultaneously require 125,000µJ and fail, but A-only or B-only each costs 62,500µJ. A/B-only sensitivity cases are researcher-forced explicit alternatives and must not be mislabeled as actual autonomous solutions.

## Prespecified outcomes, tests, acceptance

- Per archived world: time-indexed x_A,x_B,v_A,v_B; command/applied forces and link force; all feasible subsets and chosen subset/reason; every candidate work and accepted positive motor spend; F002 per-cell ledger, patch claimant/first-entry/depletion, remaining balances, each cell's own net acquired minus motor spent minus join fee; full config and source/parameter/seed SHA.
- Paired estimands: each cell's change in net energy (YY vs NN with identical commands, field, seed and budget); additional fixed no-drive outside controls separately labeled; sign categories both-positive, mixed, both-negative, neutral; changes in first resource claimant and motor admissions; number of ambiguous arbitration cases and results sensitive to arbitrator. These are descriptive bounded-model effects, not survival/evolution inference. Report 32 blockwise summaries, not pseudo-independent p-values.
- Falsifiers: dynamics and action-reaction residual outside fixed 1e-10 SI (where applicable); nonzero F002 integer µJ residual; later-in-step energy access, cross-cell credit, incomplete logging, moving field conditioned on trajectory, replay mismatch, A-first hidden priority, incorrect rejected-motor inertia, positive motor at zero balance. Reject any such evidence as invalid. Independent verifier MUST parse archived source-bound data without importing the producer and recompute dynamics, work, depletion and accounting. Rehashing forged evidence must be rejected.
- Engineering qualification: frozen pre-observation commit SHA preserved; tests on py3.11 and 3.12, full inherited F001–F020 regressions, two clean output roots byte-identical, independent verifier and adversarial mutations. No formal adoption until observed evidence plus critical review; negative science outcomes can coexist with bounded *software instrument* acceptance. No external peer review claimed.
